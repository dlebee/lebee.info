About

I've been programming since 2007 — 19 years now. I like working below the abstraction layer: understanding how distributed systems actually behave across networking, consensus, state, infrastructure and failure boundaries, then turning that into production systems.

My work spans blockchain infrastructure, protocol design and backend engineering — Layer 1 and Layer 2 architecture, EVM and Polkadot SDK / Substrate, consensus and finality, validator and RPC infrastructure, and protocol economics. I'm most interested where abstractions start leaking: concurrency, distributed state, consensus edge cases, network transport, and the gap between how a protocol is described and how it behaves in production.

As a leader I stay close to the systems: mentoring engineers, reviewing architecture, challenging assumptions, debugging across layers, and helping teams turn ambitious protocol ideas into incremental, operable software. I lean heavily on AI and agent-based workflows — spinning up ephemeral test networks, analyzing logs and artifacts, and building CI that catches integration problems before production.
