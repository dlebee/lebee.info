v1 — Blueprint & Signal

The e is the brand.

An architect's identity: warm drafting paper, deep ink, a faint blueprint grid, and a single green signal. The signal is the acute accent of Lebée — the one detail people get wrong. No logo: the name, set well, is the identity.

01 Name

The name is the identity

No logo. The name is set in Schibsted Grotesk Bold with tight tracking, and the acute accent of the second-to-last e is drawn as a green bar. That one detail carries the brand.

Setting the name

Schibsted Grotesk Bold, −3.5% tracking. Write the accented e plain and wrap it in .e-accent so the accent becomes the green bar. Small sizes (nav, footer) can use a regular é.

02 Colour

Paper, ink, one signal

Mostly paper and ink. Green is rare on purpose: links' underline, bullets, the accent, "live" status, skill bars. If a page has more than a few touches of green, remove some.

03 Type

One grotesk, one mono

Schibsted Grotesk for everything you read; JetBrains Mono for anything that's data — dates, labels, durations, section numbers. Both free on Google Fonts.

05 Voice

How it sounds

Do

  • Lead with the outcome: "let Gluwa ship on Ethereum and BNB Chain with no chain-specific code."
  • First person, plain verbs: built, led, moved, designed.
  • Name the real systems — Azure Service Bus, Substrate, Moneris.

Don't

  • Buzzword stacks ("synergistic, cutting-edge solutions").
  • More than one accent colour on a page.
  • Add a logo or icon next to the name.
